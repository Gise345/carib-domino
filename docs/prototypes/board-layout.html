The board, before and after

Both boards are the live 800 × 1730 canvas at 40%. The hatched red blocks are measured overlaps, not sketches — the top profile covers its hand by its full 140 × 180, and each side profile sits entirely inside its hand column. The green-dashed corner is the local profile and chat: identical in both boards, and included in the overlap check so a wider hand can't quietly grow into it.

Moving the top profile to the right of its hand is what buys the space. Stacked, the profile and hand needed two bands; side by side they need one, and the chain area gains 66 units of height.

The tile

True white instead of cream, bigger, with a heavier centre rule and pips grown from 18% to 22% of the short side. The depth comes back as three stacked layers, all pointing straight down: a hard dark keyline so the tile has an edge against the felt, a solid pale band beneath the body — that is the tile's own side, its thickness — and a soft cast shadow on the table under it. Shown at true relative scale.

The shadow direction is the whole trick. The current tile throws its shadow up and to the right, which puts the light source below and to the left — while the board is lit by string lights overhead. Two disagreeing light directions is what made the tiles read as stickers on the felt. Keep the depth, point it down, and the same tile reads as a thick slab sitting on the table.

Collapsed scoreboard

Collapsed, the panel should be a title bar and nothing else. Right now the body's padding is still being counted, so roughly 46 units of dead space sit above and below the word.

Why your hand is fanned

This one isn't a style choice — the arithmetic forces it. The bottom strip is bounded by the turn clock on the left (ends at x 132) and the profile-and-chat corner on the right (starts at x 658). That leaves 508 units for seven tiles.

Side by side, "much bigger" tops out at 66 units — six more than today, which is not the change you asked for. Fanning them at 84 with a 14-unit lap gets the full size back and costs 16% of each tile's right edge, clipping the outer pip column by about 3 units. The selected tile lifts clear anyway, and holding dominoes overlapped is how a hand actually looks. The alternative is moving the clock or the corner, and you've asked for both to stay.

Canvas is 800 × 1730 — reference width 800, match-width, at a 2.16 aspect. Tile pips, seat discs and the chain region are indicative; the fixes here are position and size only. Type stands in with rounded system faces — the shipping pair is Baloo 2 and Nunito.
