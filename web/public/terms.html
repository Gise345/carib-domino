Soft-launch builds are unfinished. Expect bugs, matches that end badly, features that appear and disappear between builds, and progress that is wiped without warning. Nothing you accumulate during the test — statistics, ranking, tokens, cosmetics — is guaranteed to survive into the released game.

Seats are not guaranteed

Places are limited. Submitting the form is a request, not an enrolment. We may decline or withdraw a seat at any time, and we may end the test programme whenever we choose.

Tokens are play money

In-game tokens have no real-world value. They cannot be bought during the soft launch, cannot be exchanged for money or goods, cannot be withdrawn, and cannot be transferred between players. Pose is a game of dominoes, not a gambling product, and no part of the test involves wagering anything of value.

Test builds and the invitations to them are for you. Don't redistribute the app, share your invitation, or publish screenshots, recordings, or streams of unreleased builds without asking us first. We will usually say yes — we would just like to know.

If a rule correction from you changes how a variant ships, we would like to credit you, and we will ask before doing so.

Stop whenever you like. Email hello@caribbeandominos.com to be removed from the programme and to have your signup record deleted — see the privacy notice.
